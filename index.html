Arrow Escape - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Escape ("the App") handles information when you use the game.

Arrow Escape is an offline puzzle game developed and published by PulseCraft Studio.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Escape does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, home address, or password.

The game can be played offline and does not require user registration.

2. Advertising

Arrow Escape uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information from your device for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and performance measurement.

Depending on your device settings, region, consent choices, and applicable privacy laws, this information may include:

  • Device identifiers and Advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • IP address and coarse/approximate location derived from the IP address
  • App interaction details and ad engagement data

We do not directly collect, view, or store personal identifiers through Arrow Escape.

For detailed information on how Google manages advertising data, visit:
Google Privacy Policy • How Google Uses Data From Sites and Apps

3. Rewarded Advertisements

Arrow Escape uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertising services, including optional rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements allow players to receive in-game items, such as hint coins or puzzle escapes. Watching rewarded advertisements is completely optional.

4. Device Permissions

Arrow Escape does not request access to sensitive device permissions, including your contacts, microphone, camera, calendar, precise GPS location, or personal file storage.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate corrective action.

6. Data Security and Local Storage

Arrow Escape does not maintain user accounts or profiles on cloud servers.

Game save progress, level states, and user preferences are stored locally on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the App will delete this local save data.

7. Third-Party Services

Third-party tools integrated within Arrow Escape adhere to their independent privacy standards:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage personalized advertising preferences and reset your Advertising ID via your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
