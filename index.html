Arrow Escape - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Escape ("the App") handles information when you use the game.

Arrow Escape is an offline puzzle game developed and published by YOUR DEVELOPER NAME.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Escape does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, home address, or password.

The game can be played offline and does not require registration.

2. Advertising

Arrow Escape uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information from your device for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

Depending on your device settings, region, consent choices, and applicable laws, this information may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app interaction information, and information about advertisements shown or interacted with.

We do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or other identifying information through Arrow Escape.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy

3. Google Mobile Ads

Arrow Escape uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to provide advertising services, including banner and rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements may provide in-game rewards such as additional hint or escape coins. Watching an advertisement is optional.

4. Device Information

Advertising and third-party services may process device-related information, including device identifiers and technical information, as necessary to provide advertising, security, fraud prevention, measurement, and service functionality.

Arrow Escape itself does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, calendar, or personal files.

5. Location Information

Arrow Escape does not directly request or collect your device's precise location.

Advertising providers may independently derive approximate location from information such as an IP address where permitted by applicable policies and laws.

6. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App and its third-party service providers.

Information transmitted by third-party advertising services is handled according to their respective security and privacy practices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Arrow Escape does not maintain a user account or user profile on our servers.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. Removing the App or clearing its application data may remove locally stored game information.

Data processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to their own retention and deletion policies.
